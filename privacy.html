Privacy Policy for Nectra

Last updated: September 2026

Nectra ("we," "our," or "the app") is a family budget tracking application. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

When you use Nectra, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address and display name, provided either through Google Sign-In or when you create an email/password account.
  • Financial data you enter: transactions, budget categories, amounts, dates, and descriptions that you manually add or import from bank export files (CSV, QFX, OFX, QBO).
  • Family/household data: if you join or create a family group, your membership and role within that group.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the core functionality of Nectra:

  • Displaying your budget, transactions, and spending progress
  • Syncing data in real time between members of your family group
  • Authenticating you when you sign in

Data Storage

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), a secure cloud infrastructure provider. Data is only accessible to members of your family group who have been explicitly invited.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not display ads in the app.
  • We do not use your financial data for any purpose beyond providing the app's functionality to you and your invited family members.

Bank Import Files

When you import a CSV, QFX, OFX, or QBO file, the file is processed entirely within your device's browser. Only the resulting transaction data (date, amount, description, category) that you choose to import is saved to our database — the original file itself is never uploaded or stored.

Your Control Over Your Data

You can at any time:

  • Export all of your transaction data as a CSV file from Settings → Account
  • Delete your individual account, which removes your access but preserves data for remaining family members
  • Delete your entire family's budget data permanently, if you are the family owner

Third-Party Services

Nectra uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database) — for account management and data storage. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Nectra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at nectrasupport@gmail.com.
