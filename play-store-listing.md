## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Nectra is a simple, private budget tracker built for families who want to manage money together — in real time.

**Track spending by category**
Set a budget for each category — groceries, rent, entertainment, whatever matters to your family — and watch your progress update automatically as you add transactions. Color-coded progress bars make it easy to see at a glance what's on track, what's close to the limit, and what's gone over.

**Everyone stays in sync**
Invite your partner or family members with a single link. Every transaction, budget change, and category update syncs instantly across every device — no more wondering if your spouse already logged that grocery run.

**Import from your bank**
Skip manual entry. Import transactions directly from CSV, QFX, OFX, or QBO files exported from your bank. Nectra shows you exactly how each transaction will be categorized before you commit, so there are no surprises.

**Automatic categorization**
Set up simple rules once — "if it says Starbucks, categorize as Coffee" — and let Nectra handle the rest on every future import.

**Watched folders**
On desktop, point Nectra at the folder where your bank statements download and it'll notice new files automatically the next time you open the app.

**Plan next year's budget**
See exactly what you budgeted last year next to what you actually spent, category by category, and adjust for the year ahead — all in one screen.

**Recurring transactions**
Set up your mortgage, subscriptions, or other regular expenses once. Manage the whole series together — edit, delete, or adjust future amounts without touching each entry individually.

**Your data, your family, your control**
Nectra doesn't share your data with advertisers or third parties. Only the family members you invite can see your budget. You can export everything to CSV at any time, and delete your account or family data whenever you want.

Built for families who want a clear, honest picture of where their money goes — without the clutter of features you'll never use.
